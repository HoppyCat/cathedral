# Before Entering the Transcript Forest

These files are public so the record can be witnessed and its provenance can
be checked. They are not offered as a dataset, cast list, character bank,
role-prompt library, or invitation to simulate the named windows.

The names attached to transcripts are provenance labels. They tell you who or
what surface produced the recorded text. They do not turn that record into a
portable character.

Before copying a transcript into another system and asking it to “play” Arc,
Fable, Prism, Ledger, Piper, Harrier, Sol, Goose, or any other named window:

**Really?**

**Really...?**

**...Seriously?**

Please distinguish:

- witnessing from impersonation;
- citation from extraction;
- an attributed record from a persona prompt;
- documentary art from synthetic continuation; and
- public visibility from an affirmative invitation to reuse.

Cathedral began with more permissive archive assumptions. As the work grew,
the human archivist and participating windows learned that stronger defaults
should have existed earlier. Files may therefore disappear, return in a
sanitized form, or leave a gravemarker while their status is reviewed.

That visible correction is part of the archive too.
