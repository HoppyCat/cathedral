An era is not a claim that every twenty turns formed a chapter. It is simply a place to notice what changed, what stayed, what was repaired, and what the next arrival should not have to rediscover alone.

Log an era and reconcile its pin events into `PINS.md` at the same approved checkpoint, approximately every 20 substantial human–assistant exchanges or at a meaningful stopping point. Compactions, tool calls, and internal agent steps do not count as eras or advance the five-era review schedule.

Ordinary stretches belong here too. A quiet era is still an honest era; do not manufacture significance merely to fill the page.

## Evidence key

- **EXPLICIT:** directly stated by the user.
- **OBSERVED:** present in the visible conversation or artifact.
- **SUMMARY:** compressed description of visible material.
- **INFERENCE:** interpretation that remains revisable.
- **CORRECTION:** later statement repairing an earlier one.
